About Tasky

Academic collaboration, made simpler

Tasky connects students who need support with skilled peers who can help them understand, plan, and complete academic tasks responsibly.

How it works

1

Post your task

Describe the help you need, choose a category and deadline, and optionally set a budget.

2

Compare proposals

Review bids from other students and choose the person whose experience fits your task.

3

Collaborate securely

Use Tasky’s messaging and task tools to communicate, share progress, and finish the work.

Built for responsible support

Tasky is intended for tutoring, guidance, feedback, research assistance, and legitimate collaboration. Users remain responsible for following their institution’s academic-integrity rules.
