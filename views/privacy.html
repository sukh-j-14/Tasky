Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

Tasky is a platform for posting, finding, and collaborating on tasks. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect account details you provide, such as your name, username, email address, profile information, task content, bids, and messages. If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information that you authorize Google to share, such as your name, email address, and profile identifier.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide Tasky features, display relevant profile and task information, support communication between users, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that host or operate Tasky, when necessary to provide requested features, or when required by law. Data is stored using cloud hosting and database providers with access controls appropriate to the service.

Your choices

You may choose not to use Google sign-in and create an account with email and password instead. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by contacting us.
